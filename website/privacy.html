Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Waketide is designed to respect your privacy. All alarm, timer, and AI parsing data is processed entirely on-device using local models and frameworks. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal routine data on external servers.

2. On-Device AI Processing

Our intelligent natural language parser analyzes your requests locally. We do not use third-party APIs to process your speech or text input for setting alarms and timers.

3. Device Permissions

Waketide requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Notifications: To alert you when your alarms or timers go off.
  • Microphone (Optional): To allow you to set alarms using your voice via the Ask Waketide feature.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@waketide.app.
